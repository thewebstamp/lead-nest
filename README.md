# 🚀 LeadNest

A modern lead management SaaS platform designed to help service businesses capture, qualify, and convert leads more efficiently.

---

## 🌐 Overview

LeadNest is a **demo SaaS application** that showcases how service businesses can automate their lead management pipeline—from initial contact to conversion.

It focuses on reducing missed opportunities by streamlining lead capture, qualification, and follow-up processes.

---

## 💡 Problem

Service businesses often struggle with:

- Missed follow-ups  
- Disorganized lead tracking  
- Inefficient communication  
- Low conversion rates  

---

## ✅ Solution

LeadNest provides a centralized platform that:

- Automates lead capture  
- Applies intelligent qualification rules  
- Schedules and tracks follow-ups  
- Provides a real-time dashboard for insights  

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 📥 Dynamic lead capture forms  
- 🧠 Automated lead qualification logic  
- 📧 Email templates for quick communication  
- ⏰ Follow-up scheduling system  
- 📊 Real-time dashboard for lead tracking  
- 🎨 Smooth UI with animations  

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js 15  
- **Language:** TypeScript  
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS  
- **Authentication:** NextAuth.js  
- **Database:** PostgreSQL  
- **UI & Animations:** shadcn/ui, Framer Motion  
- **Deployment:** Vercel
